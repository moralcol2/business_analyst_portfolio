Background

I spent my career in enterprise sales at Oracle and Flex Technology Group, where I built the analytical tools my territory and leadership relied on — pipeline dashboards, TCO models, and quota tracking systems. That work was always the part of the job I cared about most: turning raw activity data into decisions.

I'm now formalizing that into a data analytics career, with a focus on working alongside stakeholders and leadership to turn data into strategic decisions — the same kind of work I did at Flex when building cost models for client and executive review. I hold a B.S. in Industrial Distribution (CS & Business minors) from Texas A&M, FINRA Series 6/63/SIE licenses, and completed the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate and a Data Analytics in Healthcare Certificate in May 2026.

This portfolio uses synthetic datasets modeled on real territory structures and benchmarks from my own performance — built to demonstrate analytical approach, not to disclose proprietary employer data.
